---

## What It Is

The Azure Branch is Arbour's engineering and technical infrastructure institution — the Branch responsible for the maintenance of every system that keeps the city alive. Reactors, atmospheric processors, water recycling infrastructure, power distribution, transit systems, the hull itself: all of it falls under Azure Branch's operational mandate. It is the largest Branch by membership, the oldest in continuous unbroken function, and the one whose failure would end Arbour faster than any other single event.

Its authority over the city's technical systems is, in practice, total. No other institution has the knowledge to challenge it. No other institution wants the responsibility.

---

## The Maintenance Civilisation

Azure Branch operates on what might be called ritual engineering — procedures followed precisely because deviation causes death, without necessarily understanding the principles that make those procedures work. Its manuals are sacred texts. Its training is apprenticeship, not education. A junior member learns by working alongside a senior member, absorbing the correct sequence of actions through repetition and correction rather than through any theoretical framework that would allow independent adaptation.

This produces practitioners who are, within their documented procedures, extraordinarily reliable. It also produces an institution that is progressively less capable of responding to anything the procedures don't cover.

**Known failure states** have procedures. A coolant relay collapsed in Sector 7 in the fourth generation — the procedure for fixing it was written down, tested, and has been performed the same way every time since, regardless of whether the underlying reasoning still holds. The procedure works. Nobody asks why.

**Unknown failure states** — events with no documented precedent — are catastrophic and, when they occur, politically suppressed. The official cause of death is always something else. The Azure Branch has a dedicated sub-division whose entire function is to contain and reclassify unknown failure events before they surface publicly. This sub-division does not appear in the Branch's organisational documentation. Its existence is known within the Branch and not discussed outside it.

Each time an unknown failure is reclassified and buried, the Branch loses one more procedure it didn't know it needed. The Deviation Log — the Branch's classified internal record of every instance where a procedure failed or produced unexpected results — is the most honest document in Arbour and the most suppressed. Access requires Council clearance that most Azure Branch members don't hold. Silas Varran has been trying to access it for three years.

## Internal Structure

### Continuance Corps

The Azure Branch's reactor division, and the sub-division closest to the Branch's founding function. The Continuance Corps maintains Reactors 1, 2, and what remains of Reactor 3 — the primary power infrastructure on which the upper two thirds of Arbour depends. Its procedures are the oldest in the Branch's library and the most rigidly followed.

The Corps also maintains the access tunnels to Arbour's geothermal tap systems — tunnels that do not appear on any map available to the general population — and holds operational responsibility for the secondary fusion plant known internally as the Vault, though "operational responsibility" in this case means maintaining the seals on a collapsed access corridor and ensuring the record of what lies beyond it stays within a very small circle. The Vault's existence is known to Council leadership and a handful of senior Continuance Corps members. Its potential is not discussed.

Wren Emberlain's Azure Branch service was in the Continuance Corps, in the reactor maintenance and grid routing function. Their operational access during that period reached further than most junior members' ever does.

### The Reclassification Sub-Division

No public name. Handles the containment and official reclassification of unknown failure events — documentation, cover stories, coordination with other Branches and the medical system when the failure produced casualties requiring an alternative explanation. Its members are selected by the Branch head rather than through the standard examination and sponsorship process. They are not announced internally as being part of this function.

## The Technical Knowledge Problem

In the Sprawl, there are people who understand things the Azure Branch doesn't. Descendants of engineers, self-taught through salvage and experimentation, people who arrived at technical understanding from below — through necessity and curiosity rather than through controlled transmission — rather than from above.

This knowledge is extraordinarily valuable and extraordinarily dangerous. The Council's official position classifies unauthorised technical understanding as a form of Taint — unvetted technical experimentation causes system failures, which is sometimes true, and which also conveniently suppresses any independent understanding of how Arbour actually works.

The Azure Branch's relationship with the Sprawl's tapped conduits — illegal connections to the distribution network running through the structural fabric — is the clearest expression of how this plays out in practice. The Branch knows the conduits exist. Removing them all would cause cascading structural failures. So the conduits are tolerated, the technical knowledge maintaining them is neither acknowledged nor prosecuted, and an unofficial détente holds between the Branch and the Sprawl's self-taught engineers. The Branch loses nothing it admits to having. The Sprawl keeps the lights on.

Otis Audagar controls significant portions of the black market for technical knowledge — not just goods, but schematics, reclassified failure reports, and the names of people who know things the Branch doesn't want known.

## Inter-Branch Relations

Azure and Obsidian have historically cooperated most closely of any Branch pairing — technical infrastructure and security share obvious operational interests, and the Joint Surveillance Unit that both contribute to is the most visible expression of that cooperation. The alliance is functional rather than warm.

Azure's relationship with Verdant is periodically contentious. The Branch has filed three reports over the past century recommending a full audit and recalibration of Arbour's solar condenser systems. Verdant has blocked all three on jurisdictional grounds. The actual reason involves questions neither Branch wants asked publicly about who is responsible for what. The condensers continue to run at miscalibrated settings.

## The Twelve

At least one senior figure within Azure Branch has deep connections to the Twelve — this is established but the specific individual is not yet named. The Branch head's awareness of the Twelve's existence is an open question. The Continuance Corps' work on power suppression — maintaining the infrastructure that keeps the Vault sealed and the secondary plant's output unreported — means the most senior Corps members are, knowingly or not, executing Twelve-level policy through what appear to be ordinary operational decisions.
